    03 Before & after

    How AI changed the game

    Before · 1997

    Chess

    It took IBM a room-sized supercomputer, Deep Blue, to beat world champion Garry Kasparov. The world called it a turning point.

    Tap to see after ↻
    After · today

    Chess

    A free chess app on a phone now plays far stronger than any human ever has. Top players train with AI to find new ideas.

    Before · 2015

    Go

    The ancient board game has more possible positions than atoms in the universe. Experts thought beating a top human was still a decade away.

    Tap to see after ↻
    After · 2016

    Go

    DeepMind’s AlphaGo beat legend Lee Sedol 4–1. Its famous “move 37” looked like a mistake to experts, until it won.

    Before · 50 years

    Proteins

    Working out the 3D shape of a single protein, key to understanding disease, could take scientists years of lab work.

    Tap to see after ↻
    After · 2020 – 2024

    Proteins

    AlphaFold predicted the shapes of over 200 million proteins, almost every one known. Its creators shared the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.

    Before · 2011

    Seeing

    The best computer vision systems got about one in four photos wrong in the big ImageNet challenge.

    Tap to see after ↻
    After · 2015

    Seeing

    Deep learning cut that error to under 4%, better than the roughly 5% estimated for people. Today your phone sorts your photos by face.

    Before · 2004

    Driving

    In DARPA’s first desert race for robot cars, not one finished. The best managed about 12 km of a 240 km course.

    Tap to see after ↻
    After · today

    Driving

    Robotaxis with nobody in the driver’s seat now carry paying passengers through several US cities every day.

    Before · 1966

    Chatbots

    ELIZA, the first chatbot, mostly turned your words back into questions. Still, some people confided in it like a therapist.

    Tap to see after ↻
    After · 2022

    Chatbots

    ChatGPT was estimated to reach 100 million users in about two months, one of the fastest-growing apps in history.

    Before

    Weather

    A 10-day global forecast meant hours of number-crunching on some of the world’s biggest supercomputers.

    Tap to see after ↻
    After · 2023

    Weather

    DeepMind’s GraphCast makes a 10-day forecast in under a minute on a single machine, and beat traditional methods on most measures tested.

    Before · 2016

    Translation

    Online translators stitched sentences together phrase by phrase, which is why results often sounded robotic.

    Tap to see after ↻
    After · 2016 →

    Translation

    Neural networks began translating whole sentences at once. Today you can talk to someone in another language in near real time.

    Before · 1950

    The test

    Alan Turing asked: if you chat with a machine and can’t tell it from a person, should we say it thinks?

    Tap to see after ↻
    After · 2025

    The test

    In a study at UC San Diego, people chatting with GPT-4.5 judged it to be the human 73% of the time, more often than the real humans.
