Why beatmatch?

When two tracks play at the same speed with their kicks landing together, you can blend them for as long as you like. That smooth handover is what keeps a dance floor moving.

Listen first

Two kicks slightly out of time sound like a galloping horse: “ba-dum, ba-dum”. When they’re locked, the two kicks become one, solid and punchy.

Small moves

Real DJs fix drift with tiny touches: a small push or drag on the jog wheel, a fraction of a percent on the tempo fader. Patience beats big moves.

Cue, EQ, Sync

Cue: hold it to preview the track from your cue point, let go to jump back. EQ: turn the LOW down on the incoming track so two basslines don’t clash. Sync: matches tempo and beat for you. Handy, but learn it by ear first.
